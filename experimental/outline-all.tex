\fancyhead[R]{Test collection}
{\Huge\bfseries Delimiter rendering tests}\par\bigskip
Eight math fonts, one rendering method.\par\bigskip
Each font has three pages: target heights (12, 20, 32, 64 pt), intermediate
heights (11.5--16.5 pt), and complete formulas. Both sides of parentheses,
square brackets, braces, angles, single bars and double bars are tested.
Formulas cover scripts, multiple middle delimiters, nested niji colors and
explicit line breaks with individual equation numbers.\par\bigskip

GFS Neohellenic Math & 23--25
\end{tabular}\par\bigskip
``Native'' denotes native font glyph rendering with the same finaldelim sizing
request, not unmodified LaTeX size selection. Continuous rendering interpolates
prebuilt outlines; beyond that range it retains native assembly placement and
fills the component contours together.\par\medskip
Formula examples use q=8, decay length 20pt and scale=0.9. Controlled height
samples use q=0, scale=1, delimiterfactor=1000 and delimitershortfall=0pt.
