\noindent{\Large\bfseries Delimiter sizing: six height arrangements}\par\smallskip
\noindent XITS Math / LuaLaTeX. Four equal-width slots (36\,pt), three equal gaps (4\,pt).
Each row contains the same two tall and two low boxes; only their order changes.\par\medskip
\setbox\highbox=\hbox{$\displaystyle\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}x_k}{1+\frac pq}$}

\directlua{dofile('experimental/height-patterns.lua')}
\par\medskip\small
\noindent $r_i=\max(h_i-a,d_i+a)$ is the radius about the math axis $a$;
$H=\max_i r_i$, $L=\min_i r_i$, $E=\max(r_1,r_4)$.\par
\noindent Distance rule: $R=\max_i\{L+(r_i-L)\exp(-\delta_i/\lambda)\}$,
with $\delta_i$ the gap from slot $i$ to the nearer content boundary and
$\lambda=36+4=40$\,pt. Distances here are $0,40,40,0$\,pt.
This is an experimental rule, not a validated perceptual model.\par
\noindent Blue delimiters remain centered on the math axis. $R$ is the sizing input;
TeX applies its usual delimiter selection rules, so the rendered height need not be $2R$.
